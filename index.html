Questions

Is WordNest free?

Yes. WordNest is free and you can use everything without an account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your decks and progress are stored on your phone. An account is optional, only to back up and sync between phones.

Which languages can I learn?

Any pair: you create the decks. The app itself is in English, French and Arabic.

How does the AI work?

It is optional. You add your own free Google Gemini key; it stays on your phone and is only sent to Google.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — WordNest is on Android (Google Play) for now.
